About Rolling Stone Creamery

At The Rolling Stone Creamery, we’re a family-owned ice cream shop in Huron, Ohio serving up sweet moments for the community. We offer hand-dipped ice cream from Toft Dairy, along with toppings and specialty treats like our signature “S’mores on the Shore,” giving everyone—from classic cone lovers to adventurous dessert fans—something to enjoy. Our 23-foot Airstream trailer adds a unique touch of history and character, once traveling with The Rolling Stones on tour and now settled by the lake.

Whether you’re walking the pier, watching the boats, or enjoying a Lake Erie sunset, we’re here to make every visit a little sweeter—a place to relax, enjoy ice cream, and make memories with family and friends. Visit us at 120 Main St, Huron, OH 44839 near the Huron Pier.
